# Principles

How finch should feel to the household, and what it must never do. Check a new
report, message or command against these. Working rules for code are in
`CLAUDE.md`; what is being built is in `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md`.

- **Silent unless it matters.** A message earns its place with a change worth
  acting on. No news is no message, never a "nothing to report".
- **Euros a year first.** A finding opens with what it costs or saves a year,
  then the detail. Not percentages, not category bars.
- **Every figure traces to rows.** Any number can be expanded into the
  transactions behind it, and a chat answer can show the query it came from.
- **Unknown stays visible.** Unclassified rows, unverifiable balances and
  missing months are shown as a count to work down, never folded into a total.
- **Stale is said first.** When the ledger is out of date, that comes before
  any finding that depends on it.
- **Corrections are cheap.** Confirming or correcting a finding takes one
  reply, and the correction applies to all history at once.
- **Recommendations keep their evidence.** Source, retrieval date and what was
  compared, so a suggestion can be checked and redone.
- **Short explanations, not dashboards to watch.** What finch says is what
  changed and what to do about it. Charts wait on the page until asked for.
- **Who said so is kept.** Every document, fact and decision shows who
  added it, and every thing who owns it.
- **Both partners' privacy is theirs.** Each person decides what of theirs is
  in the household ledger and what a model may read.
